Domenico Paolo, Ph.D.

AI researcher working at the intersection of medical imaging, multimodal learning and clinical outcome prediction.

Research fellow at the University of Cassino and Southern Lazio, Italy. I develop and evaluate machine learning methods for heterogeneous clinical data, with a particular focus on lung cancer prognosis and medical image analysis.

Research agenda

Learning from complex clinical data

Medical imaging

CT representation learning, attention-based aggregation and transfer learning for lung cancer prognosis.

Clinical NLP

Named entity recognition and hierarchical representations of unstructured electronic health records.

Multimodal & survival learning

Integration of imaging, clinical text and structured variables for clinical outcomes and time-to-event prediction.

Foundation models

Exploring pretrained CT representations and their transferability to prognostic tasks.

Clinical applications

Non-small cell lung cancer, treatment response and pulmonary embolism prognosis.

Robust evaluation

External validation, experimental comparison and reproducible research pipelines.
